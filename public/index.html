Site visit Krish

A clear, repeatable walkround for you and Krish. Record what is working, fix what needs attention, and leave with named actions.

How to use: You complete the inspection, record gaps and photos, and score the site. Finish the inspection to calculate the result and create a manager link for follow-up actions. The manager completes their actions from that link.
Manager completion: For each assigned action, set Status to Done and choose Save as complete. Add an update for anything still open or in progress, then enter your name and choose Finish manager follow-up.
